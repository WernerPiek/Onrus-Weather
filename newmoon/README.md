# New Moon Abalone Farm — buyer demonstration build

A white-label variant of the Abagold Abalone Farm game (`/game/`), re-skinned
for New Moon (Goh Joo Hin) as a demonstration: New Moon red-and-gold theme,
crescent mark, can range renamed to Brine / Braised / Royale, New Moon product
facts, and the buy link pointing at the Goh Joo Hin e-shop. Gameplay, the
Hermanus/Walker Bay farm information and the engine are identical to `/game/`,
and saves are stored under separate keys so both games coexist on one device.

The crescent mark and colours here are a placeholder rendition drawn for this
demo — before any public or commercial use, obtain New Moon's approval and
replace them with their official brand assets.

Play at `/newmoon/` on the deployed site. `qr-newmoon.png` in this folder is a
print-ready QR code pointing there.
